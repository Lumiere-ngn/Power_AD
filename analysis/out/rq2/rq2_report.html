The primary RQ2 experiment compares three detector families on 833 synthetic fault episodes across 61 eligible smart bus-stop devices (Sep–Oct 2026) with a 1% false-alarm budget calibrated on July 2026 data:

  • Non-graph detectors (max|z|, Mahalanobis, Isolation Forest): best recall 50.5% (IForest) at 1% FAR
  • Engineering-informed graph detector (max|z| over graph-conditioned residuals): recall 30.1% at same FAR
  • Random DAG controls: 30 degree-preserving random graphs → mean recall 33.6% ± 5.3%, hand graph at 7th percentile
Key finding: The engineering-informed hand graph does not outperform the best non-graph detector (IForest) at the 1% FAR budget. It also does not significantly beat degree-preserving random graphs. The most impactful edges are power-supply relationships; removing them drops recall by 6.7 percentage points.

4. Shared Residual Framework

  • Context model: RQ1 LightGBM predictions (power: 200 trees/31 leaves; thermal: 500 trees/31 leaves) from March–June fit
  • Parent model: Per-device ridge regression on graph parents (fit window only)
  • Graph-conditioned residual: r = observed − predicted(node | parents)
  • Standardisation: Robust MAD scale per device/node from July calibration only
  • Graph score: max |z| across all 10 nodes (analogous to max|z| non-graph)

7. Conclusions & Limitations

7.1 Conclusions

  1. The engineering-informed hand graph does not outperform the best non-graph baseline (IForest) at 1% FAR (30.1% vs 50.5% recall).
  2. The hand graph does not significantly beat degree-preserving random DAGs (7th percentile).
  3. Power-supply edges are the only critical substructure; removing them costs 6.7 pp recall.
  4. Candidate localisation identifies cpu_gpu_power_watts as most frequent top anomaly; input_voltage_volts as most common upstream root cause.
  5. GDN overfits with default Lasso settings; requires stricter regularisation.

7.2 Limitations

  • Synthetic ground truth only: evaluation on injected faults, not independently confirmed real faults
  • Test period reuse: Sep–Oct data was previously inspected in pilot runs (Protocol D1)
  • No real root-cause labels: localisation is candidate, not confirmed
  • Hourly resolution: may miss transient faults <1h
  • GDN not production-ready: requires Lasso regularisation tuning
  • GDN learned graph has cycles (e.g., power ↔ input_power); ridge regression handles but interpretation harder

7.3 Reproducibility

All scripts accept no arguments, use fixed seeds (20261103 for data, 20261103 for GDN, 20261110 for eval), and write outputs to analysis/out/rq2/data/. 57 unit tests pass.
